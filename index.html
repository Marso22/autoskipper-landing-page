Frequently Asked Questions

Will YouTube block my video player for using this?
Absolutely not. YouTube actively penalizes traditional network blockers because they block network requests and prevent media from loading entirely. AutoSkipper is an automation tool, not a network blocker. It seamlessly interacts with the standard video player environment, making it fully compliant and undetectable by anti-adblock scripts.
How is AutoSkipper different from traditional network blockers?
Standard blockers forcefully stop video files from communicating with the server, which immediately flags your account and triggers warning popups. AutoSkipper uses a completely different, proprietary automation approach to clear interruptions instantly without breaking the platform's rules.
Will this slow down my browser?
Not at all. AutoSkipper is an incredibly lightweight extension that sits completely dormant until an interruption appears on your screen. It uses virtually zero system resources and will never slow down your daily browsing.
